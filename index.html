About StarWeaving AI

Founded with a vision to explore and expand the frontiers of artificial intelligence, StarWeaving is a research group dedicated to connect similar and intelligent minds together and build open tools that serve both science and society. We focus on areas including large language models, multi-agent systems, and theoretical foundations of machine learning. We are tech enthusiasts with a passion in AI, we are excited to share our research with other people!

AI Agents

Experiments with HF Agent Framework, smolagents, llamaindex, and langgraph. Code Agents, Tool Calling Agents. Inference Models, local LiteLLM, RAG.
